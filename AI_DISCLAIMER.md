# AI Usage Notice

## AI involvement

AI tools may be used in the development and maintenance of this project, including coding, debugging, documentation, research, and related technical tasks.

## Human oversight and responsibility

AI-assisted output is reviewed, edited, and accepted by the project maintainer before it is published. Responsibility for the final project remains with the maintainer.

## Limitations and attribution

AI assistance does not independently guarantee accuracy or reliability. This notice describes possible assistance with development and maintenance; it does not attribute authorship of the project's personal content to an AI system.
