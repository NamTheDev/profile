# Projects

This is an index of the creative and technical projects undertaken by Nam.

## Creative works

### Ashes

**Ashes** is a project recording liminal spaces and realistic horror. Everything is captured in real places by real human. *"We officially do NOT tolerate AI use in our content."* The project is hosted on [YouTube (@mertvyylab)](https://www.youtube.com/@mertvyylab). 

Check out the co-creator Unecap - Lunatic God 🇻🇳🇺🇦 on X. [x.com/sasgume](https://x.com/sasgume).

### Music

The genre of compositions Nam has written are mainly experimental music and fusion, stating: *"I experiment with music. My music isn't defined, it's a mixture of a lot of things I have consumed, experienced, and as well as putting out from within."* Releases are published on [Bandcamp](https://namchill235.bandcamp.com).

## Technology

### Active software

**Profile** is this website, a lightweight Markdown-driven self-documentation archive. It is hosted on [GitHub](https://github.com/NamTheDev/profile).

**MeditorD** is a nostalgic editor for securely saving linked documents, built with Bun, TypeScript, SQLite, Docker, and a browser-based interface. It is hosted on [GitHub](https://github.com/NamTheDev/MeditorD).

### Archived projects

**Green** is a Discord bot for managing Unecraft (Formerly known as NGCL - NamGreenCapLand) Minecraft and Discord server, hosted on [GitHub](https://github.com/NamTheDev/Green).

**FEARLESS** is an abandoned Discord bot built for automation and management in the Ruthless Fears community (disbanded), hosted on [GitHub](https://github.com/NamTheDev/FEARLESS).

**Flow** is an abandoned Bun and TypeScript testing project, hosted on [GitHub](https://github.com/NamTheDev/Flow).

**FARTLESS** is an abandoned Discord-bot project whose economy, loot, shop, inventory, and receipt systems were later merged into FEARLESS. It is hosted on [GitHub](https://github.com/NamTheDev/FARTLESS).
