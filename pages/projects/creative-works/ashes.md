### Ashes

Ashes is an audiovisual project by Nam and Unecap documenting liminal spaces and exploring realistic horror. Its footage is recorded on location without AI-generated content and published on [YouTube (@smertelnyyfootages)](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC9aLhqg5hrEtU8m1ozH2eiQ).

> *"We officially do NOT tolerate AI use in our content."*
